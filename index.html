About

Design with intention. Build with purpose.

I'm Parneet Kaur, a frontend and UI/UX designer who enjoys transforming ideas into clean, intuitive and visually engaging digital experiences.

My work combines visual design, user experience and frontend development. I create responsive websites, landing pages and interfaces while paying close attention to layout, typography, spacing, usability and visual hierarchy.

With hands-on industry experience and a strong interest in modern digital design, I continuously explore better ways to make websites both beautiful and functional.
